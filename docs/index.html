VolBoost

Turn your whole Mac up past 100%. VLC-style volume boost for every app, right from the menu bar — loud, and still clean.

Download for macOS View source

Free & open source · macOS 14.2+ · Apple silicon & Intel · ~1 MB

Louder, without the crackle

Up to 200%

Boosts every app at once — browser, Zoom, Spotify, games — using the same volume curve as VLC. That's up to +18 dB.

Stays clean

A lookahead limiter turns peaks down just before they hit, instead of clipping them. No distortion, even at full boost.

5-band EQ

Bass, Low-mid, Mid, Presence and Treble, with presets — including one tuned for small built-in speakers.

Stays out of the way

Lives in the menu bar, follows your output when you plug in headphones, adds ~16 ms of delay and ~1% CPU.

Install

  1. Download VolBoost.zip and drag VolBoost.app into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. VolBoost isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS will block the first launch — go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. Click the speaker in your menu bar and switch VolBoost on. macOS asks for System Audio Recording permission — VolBoost needs it to read the audio it boosts. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
Prefer the terminal? This removes the download quarantine flag instead of step 2: 
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VolBoost.app

How it works

VLC goes past 100% by cubing its volume and handing it to its own audio output with no upper limit — but that only works for what VLC plays. VolBoost does the same thing for your whole Mac using Core Audio process taps:

apps → tap on your output device (originals muted)
     → EQ → gain (percent³) → lookahead limiter
     → your speakers or headphones
